First release.

- Logins, cards and secure notes, with custom fields.
- Two-factor codes, and a password, passphrase and username generator.
- Vaults opened with their key or through a signer app such as Amber.
- Import and export of Bitwarden files.
- Live sync on Nostr relays, encrypted before it leaves the device.
- A lock password, with biometrics or the screen lock as a shortcut.
- Copied passwords cleared from the clipboard, screenshots blocked.
- 11 languages.
